Support

How do I load my team? On My Team, tap Add players and search for each player, or import from Sleeper with your username.

How do I set my league's scoring? Settings lets you choose points per catch, passing touchdown and interception values, and your starting lineup spots.

When do projections update? Twice a day during the season, and again late Sunday morning for injury news.

Are projections guaranteed? No. They are estimates from past game data. Each one comes with an upside and a downside.
