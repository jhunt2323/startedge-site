StartEdge Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. StartEdge has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your teams, rosters and settings are stored only on your iPhone.

Projections

The app downloads a weekly projections file from this website. The download does not include any information about you or your teams.

Sleeper import

If you choose to import a roster from Sleeper, the app sends the username you type directly to Sleeper's public service to read your league rosters. We never receive it. Sleeper's own privacy policy applies to that request.
